## 📋 General Information

[← Back to Curriculum](./index.md)


### Learning Process

- Each lesson takes approximately **2 hours**:
  - **45–50 minutes** — first part of the lesson.
  - **10–15 minutes** — break.
  - **45–50 minutes** — second part of the lesson.
  - **10–15 minutes** — Q&A, discussion, and questions related to homework or the lesson.

- The total duration of the learning program depends on the student's learning pace and progress.
  - Each module will nevertheless have a meaningful estimated duration, which will be specified separately.

- Students should complete homework and practical exercises within a reasonable timeframe according to the requirements of each lesson.

- The **first two days are free**. No payment is required during this period. These first days are intended to give the student enough time to understand the learning process and decide whether they want to continue.

### Computer & Equipment

- If a student does not have their own computer, a PC can be provided for learning at the school.
  - Currently, **3 PCs are available** for the first students.

- Students may leave their personal glass in the school kitchen and use it throughout their learning period.

- Please do not damage or misuse equipment, furniture, or other inventory belonging to the school or other people.

### Communication

- Students and teachers can communicate through the chat or messaging platform they prefer.

- If you have been working on a problem for a while and cannot find a good solution, ask the teacher or other students for help rather than spending excessive time stuck on the same problem.

- Respect other students, teachers, and their time.

- The primary language of communication is **Armenian**.

- Please use voice messages sparingly. :)

### School Rules

- Smoking is not allowed at the school.

- Please do not come to school under the influence of alcohol or other substances.

- Please avoid coming to school immediately after consuming foods with a strong smell, such as garlic. :)

- Respect the personal belongings, equipment, and workspace of other students and teachers.

### Controversial Situations

- If a situation arises that is not covered by these guidelines, it should be discussed with the relevant people at the school and resolved through communication and mutual respect.

- In situations where a final decision is required regarding the learning process, classroom rules, or school organization, **the teacher's decision takes precedence**.
